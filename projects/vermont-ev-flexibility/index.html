Vermont Flexibility Explorer

How much can flexibility flatten net demand?

Summary: One peak summer week of Vermont demand for electricity with a 300 MW data center and 250,000 EVs (~50% of vehicles). You can split weekly supply of energy between solar, wind and run-of-river hydro (the three always total 100%), then choose how much EV charging can be flexed. Total EV energy is unchanged, and net demand is never pushed past zero. Within those limits charging is placed to keep net demand as low as possible while filling the deepest valleys where renewables are abundant.
